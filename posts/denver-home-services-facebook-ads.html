Facebook & Instagram Ads for Denver Home-Service Businesses

By Christopher Root · TruAim Marketing · June 20, 2026 · 7 min read

← Back to all posts

If you run an HVAC, plumbing, or electrical business along the Front Range, you already know the leads are out there — the question is how to reach them without lighting money on fire. Facebook and Instagram are some of the best channels for Denver home-service businesses, but only if you run them like a pro instead of boosting random posts. Here's the playbook I'd use.

Lead with the offer, not your logo

Homeowners scrolling Instagram aren't looking for a brand — they're looking for a reason to act. The ads that win for home services lead with a concrete offer: a seasonal tune-up special, a free estimate, financing on a new system, or a "we'll be there today" urgency angle. Your logo and polish matter, but the offer is what stops the scroll.

Creative that looks local and real

Overly polished stock-photo ads underperform for trades. What works in Denver:

  • Real footage of your techs, trucks, and finished jobs — phone video is fine and often better.
  • Before-and-afters that show the problem you solved (a failing furnace, a flooded basement, an old panel).
  • A recognizable local feel — Colorado homes, weather, neighborhoods. People trust businesses that look like they're actually here.

Target tight, then let the platform work

You don't need hyper-complex targeting. Start with your real service area — the specific Denver-metro zip codes you'll actually drive to — homeowners, and an age range that fits your customer. Then give Meta's system room to find the people most likely to book. Over-narrowing is one of the most common ways local accounts choke off their own results.

For home services, speed-to-lead beats almost everything. The business that calls back in five minutes wins the job.

Capture the lead the right way

Decide whether you want calls or form leads, and build for it. Lead forms inside Facebook/Instagram are frictionless and great for volume; a click to a fast-loading landing page or a click-to-call can produce higher-intent leads. Whatever you choose, make sure leads hit your phone or CRM instantly — a lead that sits for an hour is often already someone else's customer.

Track what actually matters

Don't judge home-service ads on likes or even cost-per-lead alone. Track booked jobs and revenue. A $45 lead that books a $9,000 install is a home run; a $9 lead that never answers the phone is worthless. Proper conversion tracking is what lets you pour budget into the campaigns producing real jobs.

The seasonal edge in Colorado

Front Range weather is your ad calendar. Heating demand spikes with the first cold snap; AC and indoor-air-quality interest climbs in summer; storm season drives roofing and electrical. Plan creative and budget around these swings and you'll catch homeowners exactly when they're ready to call.
