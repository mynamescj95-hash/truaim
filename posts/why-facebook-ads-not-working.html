5 Reasons Your Facebook Ads Aren't Working (Denver SMB Edition)

By Christopher Root · TruAim Marketing · June 16, 2026 · 6 min read

← Back to all posts

"I tried Facebook ads and they didn't work." I hear it from Denver business owners constantly — and almost every time, the platform isn't the problem. The campaign was set up in a way that was never going to win. Here are the five reasons local ad accounts quietly burn money, and how to fix each one.

1. Your offer isn't compelling

The number-one killer. If your ad just says "We do plumbing, call us," there's no reason to act now. Strong offers give people a reason: a discount, a free estimate, a bonus, a deadline. Fix: lead with a specific, genuinely good offer and make the next step obvious.

2. Your creative blends in

People scroll past anything that looks like an ad and feels generic. Stock photos and corporate polish underperform. Fix: use real, native-feeling creative — your team, your work, your customers — and test several versions instead of betting on one.

If your ad could be any business in your industry, it's invisible. Specific beats slick.

3. Your tracking is broken

If your pixel and conversion tracking aren't set up correctly, Meta is optimizing blind — and you can't tell which ads actually produce customers. Fix: get conversion tracking installed and verified before you scale a dime. This one mistake quietly wastes more Denver ad budgets than any other.

4. Your budget is too small to learn

Spending $5 a day and expecting results is like planting a garden and watering it once. The platform needs enough data to find your buyers. Fix: fund the campaign at a level that lets it gather real data — for most local businesses that's a few thousand a month — or narrow your goal so a small budget can still produce signal.

5. You're not following up on leads

Ads can deliver leads all day, but if nobody calls them back fast, they're wasted. Speed-to-lead is everything, especially for home and local services. Fix: route leads straight to your phone or CRM and respond within minutes, not hours.

The bottom line

Facebook and Instagram ads absolutely work for Denver small businesses — when the offer, creative, tracking, budget, and follow-up are all stacked correctly. Miss one and the whole thing underperforms, which is why "ads don't work" usually means "this setup didn't work."
